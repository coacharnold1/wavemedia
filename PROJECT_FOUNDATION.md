## Original Vision Document (from ChatGPT planning)

### 1. Overall Vision
We are building a digital headquarters for WAVE Media—not just a portfolio site.

The website should operate as:
- Luxury production-company portfolio
- Client acquisition platform
- Automated booking system
- Eventually an online education business

Position WAVE Media as a high-end creative company capable of serving individuals, corporations, athletic organizations, schools, and entertainment clients.

### 2. Website Structure (8 Primary Sections)

1. **Homepage** - Cinematic opening video, WAVE branding, featured projects, service highlights, prominent booking buttons
2. **About WAVE** - Company story, founder biography, professional experience, documentary film credits, creative philosophy
3. **Cinematography Portfolio** - Commercial productions, documentaries, music videos, interviews, promotional films, branded content
4. **Photography Portfolio** - Sports portraits, media days, events, corporate headshots, lifestyle photography, commercial work
5. **Services & Packages** - Service descriptions, starting prices/custom quotes, production deliverables
6. **Booking & Client Portal** - Project inquiries, scheduling, contracts, deposits, questionnaires, client communication
7. **WAVE Academy** - Online courses: photography, cinematography, editing, lighting, content creation (future phase)
8. **Contact & Business Inquiries** - Contact form, social media, business collaborations, consultation requests

### 3. Visual Design & Branding

**Premium cinematic appearance** (not standard photography template):
- Black and charcoal backgrounds
- Gold accents
- Modern typography
- Cinematic motion effects
- Large photographs and videos
- Responsive: desktop, tablets, phones equally well

### 4. Automated Business Operations

**Ideal client experience flow**:
1. Client visits wavemedianj.com
2. Reviews portfolio and services
3. Submits project inquiry
4. Schedules consultation
5. Receives proposal and contract
6. Pays deposit securely
7. Receives completed deliverables

**Goal**: Reduce manual emails, missed inquiries, payment delays, administrative work via client-management system integration

### 5. Revenue Opportunities

| Stream | Opportunity |
|--------|------------|
| Cinematography | Commercials, documentaries, interviews, branded films |
| Photography | Sports, portraits, media days, corporate & event photography |
| Event Production | Weddings, celebrations, conferences, corporate events |
| Monthly Retainers | Recurring video and social media content |
| Sports Media | Athletic department packages, recruiting films, team content |
| WAVE Academy | Online courses, educational resources, editing tutorials |

**Initial Priority**: Booking paid production work  
**Second Stage**: WAVE Academy after core website is operating

### 6. Development Phases

**Phase 1 — Brand and Foundation** (1–2 weeks)
- Domain configuration, temporary landing page, branding, content collection, website architecture, visual design

**Phase 2 — Core Website Launch** (2–4 weeks)
- Homepage, portfolio galleries, service pages, About section, inquiry forms, mobile testing, publish

**Phase 3 — Business Automation** (1–3 weeks)

**Phase 4 — Growth and Education** (future)

**Overall Timeline**: 4–6 weeks for initial launch (depending on platform, content readiness, integrations, development resources)

## Technical Decisions & Recommendations

### Project Scope
Build the website platform to serve as foundation for:
1. ✅ Website platform (PHASE 1 - THIS WORK)
2. Detailed project plan
3. Business automation integration
4. Content strategy & portfolio curation
5. SEO and marketing

### Technology Decisions Confirmed

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **Framework** | Next.js (React-based, full-stack) | Cinematic portfolio optimization, built-in media handling, full-stack capabilities, great performance & SEO |
| **Hosting** | GoDaddy | Client preference; Next.js deployable via GoDaddy hosting |
| **Booking/CRM** | Third-party (HoneyBook, Acuity Scheduling, or similar) | Reduces backend complexity, handles automation, scheduling, contracts, payments |
| **Backend** | Lightweight integration layer | Forms → HoneyBook API; contact submissions → email service |
| **Database** | Minimal (mostly via HoneyBook) | CRM handles client data; website focuses on portfolio & inquiry generation |
